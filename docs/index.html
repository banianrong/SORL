01 / ABSTRACT

A latent space should be smooth nearby—and expansive everywhere.

Effective latent diffusion requires two properties that are often treated separately: local smoothness for stable reconstruction and global dispersity for diverse generation. Existing approaches can over-concentrate representations while optimizing smoothness.

SORL takes a bottom-up route. Instead of imposing a global prior, it lets structure emerge from simple local interactions. Attraction creates coherent neighborhoods; repulsion prevents collapse. Together they form a latent manifold that improves reconstruction fidelity and generative coverage.
